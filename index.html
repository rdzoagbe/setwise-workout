Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your profile, routines and history are saved in your browser on this device. There is no sign-up, email or password.

What if I clear my browser data or change phone?

Because your data lives only on your device, clearing this site's data deletes it. Use Settings & backup → Export in the app to save a copy, and import it on your new device.

Does Setwise send my data anywhere?

Your training data is never uploaded. When you're online, the app downloads exercise illustrations from the RepDB catalog hosted on GitHub; those requests contain none of your training data, but like any web request they reach GitHub's servers.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. In Chrome or Samsung Internet, open the app and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari's Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.

Is the plan medical advice?

No. Setwise suggests starting templates. If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a qualified professional before you start, and stop any movement that causes pain.
